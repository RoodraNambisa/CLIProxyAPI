- Without a dedicated login proxy, login, OAuth refresh and Session refresh constructed `NewClient` with a zero transport timeout. The dependency's ordinary HTTP CONNECT dialer installs `time.Now().Add(0)` as a socket deadline, causing immediate failure.
- These three credential-acquisition paths now use the existing acquisition client and its tracked proxy dialer. Both refresh paths also close their owned active acquisition connections on return, as login already does.
